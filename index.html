About Me

Hi, I'm Jeremy Kebaso. I'm a passionate athlete who lives for two things above all: the thrill of a fast rally on the tennis court and the calm focus of gliding through the water. Sport keeps me disciplined, curious, and always striving to be a little better than yesterday.

Whether I'm serving under the sun or diving into an early-morning training session, I bring the same energy: show up, work hard, and enjoy every moment. Beyond competing, I love coaching juniors, analyzing match footage, and helping teammates find their rhythm.
